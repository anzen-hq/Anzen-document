# The Vision: Protecting the Trillion-Dollar Frontier

Capital is Ready, but the Infrastructure is Fragile.

* **The Audit Fallacy:** Traditional security (CertiK/Nethermind) focuses on code. It misses execution and governance failures (e.g., the $285M Drift Protocol hack in April 2026 was a governance-layer compromise, not a code bug).
* **Capital Downtime:** When exploits or market failures occur, users wait 12+ months for reimbursement. Capital is locked, and trust is destroyed.
* **Operational Friction:** On general-purpose chains, gas volatility and probabilistic finality prevent "Atomic Insurance". The ability to pay out a loss in the same minute it happens.

The DeFi market in 2026 has reached a $140 billion ecosystem, but the total addressable market (TAM) for on-chain finance, including B2B payments, tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), and institutional treasuries, is projected to reach $100 trillion.

The primary barrier to this massive capital inflow is Unquantifiable Risk.

Anzen Protocol’s vision is to maximize this market by providing the first comprehensive Insurance-as-a-Service layer. We don't just protect users/ defi eco from hacks; we ensure the utility of their capital, transforming DeFi from a "gamble" into a "guaranteed" institutional settlement rail.&#x20;
