# The Malachite Consensus Engine

Anzen leverages Arc’s bespoke consensus layer powered by Malachite, a high performance Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) implementation of the Tendermint protocol to achieve deterministic, sub-second finality



* **Deterministic Sub-Second Finality:** Arc achieves block finality in less than 350 milliseconds with 20 validators. This eliminates "probabilistic" risks and reorganizations, ensuring Anzen's insurance payouts are irreversible the moment they are triggered.
* **Throughput:** Benchmarks show the network handles 3,000+ transactions per second (TPS) on commodity hardware, scaling up to 10,000+ TPS for smaller validator sets.
* **Institutional Alignment:** The Proof of Authority (PoA) model utilizes known, regulated institutions as validators, providing the operational excellence required for financial market infrastructure.
