# FAQ & Disclaimer

* Q: Does this only support Hyperliquid? Yes, this scope is specifically integrated with Hyperliquid’s L1 SDK for real-time tracking.
* Q: Why isn't coverage always 100%? To ensure sustainable payouts. By capping the pool at 60% of premiums, the protocol can survive **"Black Swan"** market crashes that would bankrupted traditional insurers.
* Q: Is the premium refundable? No, premiums are immediately committed to the payout pool and treasury upon purchase.
* Q: How long is the settlement delay? Settlement typically takes only a few minutes after epoch-close to ensure all final trade data is synchronized.



Insurance does not guarantee trading profits. Users remain subject to contract risk, Oracle/API latency, and systemic parameters. Please review the full legal documentation and risk monitors within the Anzen app before participation.
