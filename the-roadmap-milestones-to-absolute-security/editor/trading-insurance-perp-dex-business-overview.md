# Trading Insurance (Perp-DEX) — Business Overview

#### Problem Statement

When utilizing high leverage, traders face the systemic risk of massive capital depletion within extremely short timeframes. Anzen’s Trading Insurance allows users to pay a **`fixed Premium before trading`**

<kbd><mark style="color:$success;">**`If they incur a Net Realized Loss from eligible trades within a specific time cycle (Epoch), they receive compensation according to protocol rules.`**<mark style="color:$success;"></kbd>



#### Core Differentiators

| **Feature**         | **Description**                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Custom Fees         | Users select a **`Premium`** and a **`Maximum Coverage Cap`** within tiered plan limits.                                                               |
| Epoch Cycles        | Typically \~24 hours. Only trades opened after insurance purchase and closed within the same epoch are eligible.                                       |
| Payout Liquidity    | A major portion of premiums is aggregated into a dedicated Payout Pool to facilitate claims.                                                           |
| Systemic Resilience | Operates on a Shared Risk model. If total claims exceed the pool, payouts are distributed pro-rata based on premiums paid, ensuring protocol solvency. |
