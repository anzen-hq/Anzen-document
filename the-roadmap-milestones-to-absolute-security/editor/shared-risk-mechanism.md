# Shared Risk Mechanism

#### SURPLUS State (Fully Funded)

When the total valid claim demand is less than or equal to the available Payout Pool, every eligible user receives 100% of their claimable loss (up to their coverage cap).

#### DEFICIT State (Premium-Weighted Payout)

When total claims exceed the Payout Pool, the system allocates funds based on Premium Contribution. Users who paid higher premiums receive a larger absolute share of the pool. This ensures the protocol never goes bankrupt and remains a "Blu-ray" standard for reliability.
