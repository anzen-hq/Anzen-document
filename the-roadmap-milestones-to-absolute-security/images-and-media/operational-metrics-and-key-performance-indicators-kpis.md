# Operational Metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

To maintain the economic health of the protocol, Anzen tracks a series of critical KPIs :

* **Gross Premiums per Match:** The total revenue generated from insurance sales.
* **Draw Frequency:** The percentage of matches ending in a draw, serving as a primary driver of protocol profit.
* **Pool Utilization Rate:** The percentage of collected premiums that are actually distributed as compensation.
* **VIP Conversion Rate:** The percentage of insured users who opt for the <kbd><mark style="color:yellow;">VIP Pass.<mark style="color:yellow;"></kbd>
* **Claim Rate:** The percentage of eligible users who actually perform the on-chain claim transaction.
* **Black Swan Frequency:** The statistical occurrence of extraordinary match results.
* **Average Payout Ratio:** The mean percentage of position value returned to users across all compensation events
