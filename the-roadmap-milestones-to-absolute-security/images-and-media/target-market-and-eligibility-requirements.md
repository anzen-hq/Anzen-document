# Target Market and Eligibility Requirements

The primary user base for <kbd><mark style="color:$danger;">Anzen Double Bet<mark style="color:$danger;"></kbd> consists of active <kbd><mark style="color:blue;">Polymarket traders<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd> who have already committed capital to football moneyline positions. To maintain the integrity of the insurance pool and ensure operational efficiency, the protocol enforces strict eligibility criteria.

1. **Market Type:** Users must hold `"NO"` shares in a 3-way moneyline football market on <kbd><mark style="color:blue;">`Polymarket`<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>.
2. **Minimum Position Value:** To qualify for coverage, the user's position must have a minimum valuation of your bet. The actual threshold may be adjusted dynamically based on the current market price of the `"NO"` shares.
3. **Temporal Restriction:** Insurance policies must be secured before the 45th minute of the match (halftime). This prevents "late-game" hedging where the match outcome is significantly more certain, which would otherwise introduce an adverse selection risk for the protocol.
