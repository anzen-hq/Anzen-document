# Pillar 5: The Engine: Active AI Insurance Agents

Anzen’s core strength lies in its Active Insurance AI Agents. These agents scan billions of on-chain activities to detect anomalies _before_ they become disasters.

* Anomaly Detection: Our AI was designed specifically to identify patterns like the **"CVT Fake Token"** staging that led to the Drift hack. It monitors multisig activity, governance proposals, and oracle health in real-time.
* Immediate Preventive Measures: If an AI agent detects an unauthorized withdrawal or a **"Zero-Timelock"** exploit, it issues a preventive halt and provides immediate compensation to users. AI will always be active, ensuring that users are made whole before the project team even has time to draft an "incident report."
